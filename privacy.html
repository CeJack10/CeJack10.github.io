Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026.

Studio-Upload accesses only the YouTube channels of its owner, with his explicit consent given on Google's authorization screen.

Data accessed

Video files, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, subtitles and publication settings of the owner's videos; statistics of the owner's channels (views, watch time, traffic sources).

Use

This data is used only to publish and manage the owner's own videos and to analyse the performance of his own channels. It is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Storage

Authorization tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's personal computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Studio-Upload's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy.
